## Initiatives covered

### 1. Pre-order Milestones 3 & 4 (in-flight spillover)
Automate OMFP data flow and automatic removal of sold-out pre-order stock from online (today: Digital Ops manual PIM switches). Scale pre-orders ~30 → ~300/year. PDC supports testing/hypercare; dependencies on Content Intelligence, Informatica, and an open defect path.

### 2. App Exclusive Offers
App-first (~48 hour) early access for pre-orders to drive app adoption. PDC enables early product availability via PIM sales-effective datetime; App / PE should lead the end-to-end experience. Ownership contested — Laura agrees PDC may not own E2E.

### 3. Product Data Enrichment (Ocula) — PDC lead
Kmart-first Wesfarmers POC: feed products to Ocula → human review (Trade/Content) → enrich attributes → GMC first, then Constructor / Vertex / Feedonomics. Open architecture question: write back to PIM vs Product Service. Major T1 capacity (~dev pair).

### 4. Pre-order Multi-variant (apparel) — stretch
Move pre-order attributes from product (parent) to SKU/variant so merch can selectively pre-order (e.g. AU + NZ World Cup jerseys only). Cross-system blast radius (cart, cache, OFP, storefronts). High value; capacity-dependent stretch.

### 5. Marketplace Category Mapping — hand back
Automate marketplace (Merkle/Target) category → Kmart taxonomy mapping. Appeared on PDC discovery board, but Marketplace (Divya) should own validation; Laura agreed to comment and reassign.

### 6. Black Friday Everyday Deals — AK dependency
~10 days pre-BF: 2 deals/day until allocation exhausted or window ends. Reuse pre-order automation pattern with different timings/badges. AK leads experience; PDC enables data/workflow. May force roadmap trade-offs.

### 7. Christmas Matching Outfits — AK dependency
Cross-category discoverability for matching prints (e.g. family Christmas pyjamas on PDP across mens/womens/kids). AK leads UX; PDC enables linking attributes/relationships. Scope still in discovery.

### 8. Kmart Paid Search / GMC feed — swap candidate
Initially no capacity; Laura flagged possible mandatory AK timelines that could swap with App Exclusive. Needs requirement discovery before sizing.
